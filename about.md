I am an M.S. Research student in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. As part of [Team Chiron](https://teamchiron.ai/), I work on the DARPA Triage Challenge, focusing on SWaP-constrained UAV exploration for casualty identification and vision-language reasoning for triage assessment in disaster-response scenarios.

Previously, I was a Design Engineer at [Caterpillar](https://www.caterpillar.com/), where I contributed to improving the quality of large-scale mining vehicles.

## Research Focus

- **SWaP-constrained autonomy**: Efficient UAV exploration designed for constrained disaster-response environments.
- **Vision-language reasoning for triage**: Multimodal reasoning for assessing casualty conditions and supporting triage decisions.
